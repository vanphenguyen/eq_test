## Description (Mô tả)
Nơi nhập: trang phiên bản.

```
EQ Test helps you reflect on how you recognize, manage and respond to emotions in everyday life.

Start with a 3 to 5 minute Quick Test. It has 12 questions, each one a statement about your usual behavior or a short everyday scenario. You get four area scores and an overall EQ Reflection Index, with a short reading and a few ideas to try.

Go deeper with Premium, a one-time purchase that stays with your Apple account. The Full Assessment has 40 questions and a detailed result: strengths, growth opportunities and a 7-day practice plan with short, optional activities.

FOUR AREAS
• Self-Awareness: noticing and naming your own feelings
• Self-Regulation: pausing and choosing a response when emotions run strong
• Social Awareness: noticing the cues and perspectives of others
• Relationship Management: listening, speaking up respectfully and repairing after disagreements

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your answers stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. You can delete all your data in one tap.

IMPORTANT
EQ Test is a self-reflection tool for personal learning. Its scores are product-defined reflection indices. They are not a clinical, diagnostic or validated measure of emotional intelligence, and they are not a substitute for professional support.
```
