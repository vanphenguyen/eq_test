# Question Bank and Content Specification

## EQ Test 1.0 -- Draft for expert review

**Important:** This is original MVP draft content, not a validated
psychometric instrument. Have a qualified reviewer assess content
validity, cultural suitability and potential bias before release. Do not
claim the questions constitute a scientifically validated EQ test.

## 1. Content rules

-   Keep prompts short, clear and behavior-focused.
-   Ask about typical behavior, not an idealized self-image.
-   Avoid double-barrelled statements.
-   Avoid assumptions about gender, culture, relationship status or
    employment.
-   Situational choices should be plausible and non-stigmatizing.
-   Correct/constructive options are product-authored learning keys, not
    universal truths.
-   Localize meaning, not just literal words.
-   Do not alter IDs or scoring keys during translation.

## 2. Dimensions

-   `SA`: Self-Awareness
-   `SR`: Self-Regulation
-   `SO`: Social Awareness
-   `RM`: Relationship Management

## 3. Response formats

### Self-report

Use the same five-point scale: 1 Strongly disagree 2 Disagree 3 Neither
agree nor disagree 4 Agree 5 Strongly agree

For reverse-scored items, adjusted value = 6 - selected value.

### Situational

Each item has four responses A--D. The selected option maps to a keyed
reflection score from 1 to 4. A score of 4 means the response most
closely matches the constructive behavior emphasized by this app; it
does not establish an objectively correct answer for all contexts.

### Quick Test situational options and keys

**Q-SA-03** - A (score 1): Send a sharp reply so the friend knows you
are annoyed. - B (score 2): Ignore the feeling and continue the day
without thinking about it. - C (score 4): Pause and consider what about
the message triggered the irritation. - D (score 3): Ask someone else
whether the message seems irritating. Preferred key: C=4, D=3, B=2, A=1.

**Q-SR-03** - A (score 4): Pause, lower your voice and suggest a brief
moment to regroup. - B (score 2): Speak louder so your point is not
overlooked. - C (score 3): Stay silent for the rest of the discussion
without explaining why. - D (score 1): Make a personal remark about the
other person. Preferred key: A=4, C=3, B=2, D=1.

**Q-SO-03** - A (score 2): Assume the colleague is uninterested. - B
(score 4): Avoid jumping to conclusions and, if appropriate, check in
privately. - C (score 1): Call attention to the change in front of
everyone. - D (score 3): Give them space while remaining open to hearing
from them. Preferred key: B=4, D=3, A=2, C=1.

**Q-RM-03** - A (score 2): Explain immediately why they should not have
been hurt. - B (score 1): Pretend nothing happened. - C (score 4):
Acknowledge the impact, listen and offer a sincere apology. - D (score
3): Give them time, then ask whether they would like to talk. Preferred
key: C=4, D=3, A=2, B=1.

## 6. Item distribution validation

Quick Test: - 12 total. - 8 self-report. - 4 situational. - 3 per
dimension: 2 self-report + 1 situational.

Full Assessment: - 40 total. - 24 self-report. - 16 situational. - 10
per dimension: 6 self-report + 4 situational.

## 7. Localization requirements

Maintain one canonical item ID with locale-specific prompt and option
strings: - `en`: source wording in this document. - `vi`: professionally
translated Vietnamese wording.

Do not translate scoring keys into different numeric values. Review
translated scenario choices to ensure the same relative construct and
keyed behavior are preserved.

## 8. Content review checklist

Before release: - Qualified reviewer assesses construct relevance. -
Reviewers check ambiguity and double-barrelled items. - Cognitive
interviews confirm users understand prompts as intended. - Check
scenario options for cultural assumptions. - Verify reverse-scored
statements are not confusing. - Confirm no item implies diagnosis or
moral judgment. - Verify Vietnamese and English semantic equivalence. -
Pilot-test completion time and item non-response. - Document all edits
with content version increments.
